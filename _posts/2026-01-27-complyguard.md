I’ve always been drawn to work that feels meaningful, work that contributes to building a safer and more responsible world. That’s what made ComplyGuard’s mission resonate with me from the start.

ComplyGuard is a comprehensive B2G / B2C SaaS platform designed to support regulatory agencies within the gambling industry. It enables centralized monitoring of gaming operations, helping regulators streamline compliance, enforce legal standards, and gain real-time visibility through technology-driven solutions.

I initially joined ComplyGuard as a Data and Business Intelligence Analyst, working across reporting, analytics, and regulatory data requirements. Through gaining a deeper understanding of the platform, its data ecosystem, and the operational challenges faced by the business, my role evolved into a broader engineering-focused position, leading to my promotion to Data Engineer.

Today, I work at the intersection of data engineering, regulation, and real-world impact, designing, building, and maintaining scalable data solutions that support operational monitoring, revenue reporting, tax compliance, responsible gaming, player behaviour analysis, KYC, anti-money laundering, and licensing requirements.

I work across the full data lifecycle, from data ingestion and transformation through to analytical modelling and reporting layers. I design and develop reliable data pipelines, build structured data models, optimise data workflows, and ensure that complex datasets are transformed into accurate, trusted, and accessible information for business users, regulators, and internal teams.

A key part of my role is developing robust data foundations that enable scalable reporting and analytics. This involves working with large and diverse data sources, implementing data quality checks, improving pipeline reliability, and creating maintainable solutions that support evolving business and regulatory requirements.

I collaborate closely with technical and non-technical stakeholders and teams to understand complex business processes and translate them into efficient data architectures and models. By bridging the gap between technical systems and business needs, I help ensure that data is not only accurate and performant but also meaningful and actionable.

My work also involves improving automation across reporting processes, validation workflows, and regulatory data operations, reducing manual effort while increasing consistency, transparency, and reliability.

At its core, my role is about engineering the systems that turn fragmented and complex data into dependable intelligence. It’s about building scalable solutions that help organisations make better decisions, strengthen compliance, and create a safer and more responsible industry.

More information at [Complyguard](https://www.complyguard.com/)
